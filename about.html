Who teaches at Ad1.tv?

All sessions are led by industry professionals with real-world experience in video editing, photo editing, and generative AI. You are learning directly from someone who works in the field.

How long are sessions and how often?

Sessions are 90 minutes. Once a week is recommended — enough time to go deep each session, and enough time between sessions to practice before the next one. Available 7 days a week at 9am, 1pm, and 6pm PST.

What skill level do I need to start?

None. Ad1.tv teaches beginners through advanced students. Every lesson is built around where you are right now and where you want to go — no fixed curriculum, just your goals.

What software does Ad1.tv teach?

Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Premiere Pro, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, ComfyUI, AI apps and automation, and vibe coding. Video editing, photo editing, and generative AI — all covered.

Can I learn generative AI tools like ComfyUI?

Yes. Ad1.tv teaches generative AI workflows in ComfyUI including Stable Diffusion, Flux, Wan 2.2, LTX 2.3, and Qwen models. Sessions are live and one-on-one, so you learn by doing in real time with an instructor on your own setup.

Is Ad1.tv available outside Los Angeles?

Yes. All sessions are online via Zoom or Google Meet. Ad1.tv serves clients nationwide across the United States — your location does not matter.
